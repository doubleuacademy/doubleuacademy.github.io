05 · Questions

Good to know.

A few simple answers for parents and learners before choosing a course.

What age groups are covered? ＋

The flyer groups learning into Digital Discovery for ages 6–10 and Programming Courses for ages 10–18. Individual course placement can depend on the learner's experience.

Does the program only teach coding? ＋

No. The flyer also highlights digital content creation, robotics, web design, drawing, video editing, AI, data science, VR/AR and other digital skills.

Can beginners start? ＋

The visual-coding pathway is designed to make programming concepts approachable before learners move into text-based languages and more advanced projects.

What should I do next? ＋
